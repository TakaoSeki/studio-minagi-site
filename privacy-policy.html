Privacy Policy

This policy explains how STUDIO MINAGI (the Developer) handles information in all of its iOS and Android apps and related services. It covers 経理課長の簿記仕訳 (KariKashi), ふぐポン (Fugu Pon), and apps we release in the future. Features and service providers vary by app, platform, and version. Descriptions that name a particular app or platform apply to that app or platform.

Advertising Services

Apps that display ads may use Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource Ads, Google AdMob, or other advertising partners, depending on the app. KariKashi for Android uses Unity LevelPlay for ad mediation and integrates Unity Ads through LevelPlay. The integration includes Unity Ad Quality, which may process information about ads and how users interact with them when the service is active.

Advertising SDKs can collect information directly from the app or device. Depending on the service and configuration, this may include:

  • Advertising identifiers, including the IDFA on iOS and the Android advertising ID (AAID), and other device or app identifiers.
  • Device model, operating system, app version, IP address, and approximate location inferred from information such as IP address.
  • Ad impressions, views, taps and other ad interactions, and technical data such as errors when an ad loads or displays.
  • App usage information, such as session or usage time, when the applicable measurement feature is active.

Providers may use this information to deliver ads, personalize them where permitted, measure and improve advertising, maintain service performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and support security and compliance. Unity and participating ad networks may receive this information directly from their SDKs. Advertising providers may share relevant information with partners that deliver, measure, or verify ads, as described in their privacy policies. The in-app purchases section below also explains purchase measurement by Unity Ads on Android.

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, including its advertising services
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy, including Google advertising services where used

In-App Purchases

Apps that offer in-app purchases use the applicable store. Apple processes App Store payments on iOS and Google processes Google Play payments on Android. The stores handle payment credentials such as card numbers and bank account details under their own policies. The Developer and the app do not receive or store those credentials through the store billing flow.

The app receives purchase information needed to verify entitlement, deliver the purchased content, and restore access. In KariKashi, this can include the purchased product and its status, along with transaction identifiers and store receipt data. On Android, the app uses Google Play Billing through Unity IAP to check eligible purchases automatically, including when the store connection is established, and when the user requests restoration. The billing library passes the purchase token to Google Play when acknowledging an eligible purchase. The app stores the entitlement status on the device so the purchased questions can be made available. On iOS, requests to restore purchases are handled through the App Store.

When purchase measurement is enabled for KariKashi for Android, the Unity Ads SDK can automatically obtain information about new purchases or eligible past purchases from Google Play Billing and send it to Unity Ads for advertising measurement, campaign optimization, and analysis. This may include the product ID, purchase time, order ID, purchase status, product details, and Google Play purchase data, which may contain a purchase token and a signature used for verification. This may happen when the SDK initializes or receives a purchase update. Ad networks and measurement partners may receive information as described in Unity's privacy policy.

  • App Store & Privacy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play order history
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Analytics and Diagnostics

Depending on the app, analytics and diagnostic services may include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or other providers. These services may receive usage data, device information, logs, errors, crash reports, and performance measurements to help improve stability and investigate problems.

KariKashi for Android uses Unity Engine Diagnostics. Unity may receive crash reports, reports of the app becoming unresponsive, exception logs, and technical information such as the app version, operating system, and performance data. Its advertising and billing SDKs may also send diagnostic events. Google Play Billing may send Google information about billing API results and connection issues. These diagnostics help the respective providers and the Developer monitor reliability, investigate errors, and improve their services.

App Tracking Transparency and Advertising Choices

On iOS and iPadOS, apps may ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission when applicable. The IDFA is accessible only with permission where the platform requires it. You can change your tracking choice in the device settings. If you decline tracking, you can continue to use the app and may still see non-personalized ads.

On Android, you can manage the AAID in your device's privacy or ads settings, including resetting or deleting it where supported. This changes how the identifier can be used. Ads and other processing necessary for features such as diagnostics or purchase restoration may continue.

広告SDKは、アプリや端末から直接情報を取得する場合があります。サービスや設定に応じて、次の情報が対象になります。

  • iOSのIDFAやAndroid広告ID（AAID）などの広告識別子、およびその他の端末・アプリの識別子
  • 端末の機種、OS、アプリのバージョン、IPアドレス、IPアドレスなどから推定されるおおよその位置
  • 広告の表示・閲覧・タップなどの操作情報、および広告の読み込み・表示エラーなどの技術情報

広告サービス提供者は、広告配信、許可される場合の広告のパーソナライズ、広告効果の測定・改善、サービスの品質維持、不正利用の検知・防止、安全管理や法令遵守のためにこれらの情報を利用する場合があります。Unityや配信に参加する広告ネットワークは、SDK経由で情報を受け取る場合があります。広告サービス提供者は、各社のポリシーに従い、配信・計測・検証を担う提携先に必要な情報を提供する場合があります。Android版のUnity Adsによる購入計測は、次の「アプリ内課金について」に記載しています。

アプリ内課金について

アプリは、購入権利の確認、購入済みコンテンツの提供、復元に必要な購入情報をストアから受け取ります。KariKashiでは、購入した商品や購入状態、取引識別子、ストアのレシート情報が含まれる場合があります。Android版はUnity IAPを通じてGoogle Play Billingを使用し、ストアへの接続時などに購入済み情報を自動で照会します。利用者が復元を操作した際にも照会します。対象の購入を承認する際、課金ライブラリは購入トークンをGoogle Playに渡します。アプリは購入済みの追加問題を利用できるよう、端末に購入権利の状態を保存します。iOS版での復元操作はApp Storeを通じて処理します。

KariKashiのAndroid版で購入計測が有効な場合、Unity Ads SDKがGoogle Play Billingを通じて、新規購入や照会可能な購入済み取引に関する情報を自動で取得し、広告効果の測定、キャンペーンの最適化、分析のためUnity Adsへ送信することがあります。送信情報には、商品ID、購入日時、注文ID、購入状態、商品情報、購入トークンや検証用の署名を含むGoogle Playの購入データが含まれる場合があります。取得は広告SDKの初期化時や購入状態の更新時に行われる場合があります。Unityのポリシーに従い、広告ネットワークや計測事業者に関連情報が提供される場合があります。

App Tracking Transparency（ATT）と広告設定について

iOS・iPadOSでは、必要に応じてAppleのApp Tracking Transparency（ATT）を通じてトラッキング許可を求めます。OSが許可を必要とする場合、許可したときにIDFAを利用できます。トラッキングの許可は端末の設定で変更できます。トラッキングを許可しない場合もアプリを利用でき、非パーソナライズ広告が表示されることがあります。

Androidでは、端末のプライバシー設定や広告設定でAndroid広告IDを管理できます。対応する端末では広告IDをリセットまたは削除できます。広告IDの設定を変更した後も、広告表示、診断、購入の復元などの処理が続く場合があります。

  • Appleのプライバシー設定（アプリのトラッキングを含む）
  • GoogleのAndroid広告IDに関する案内
